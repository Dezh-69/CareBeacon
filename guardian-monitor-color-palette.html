Notes: Primary blue was deepened from #3D6FE0 to #2F5480 for a calmer, more clinical feel that recedes behind the status colors. Keep blue reserved for actions/navigation only — let green/amber/red carry all status meaning so alerts stay easy to scan. Avoid pure black (#000) and pure white (#FFF) for text and background respectively; use the near-black and off-white shades above instead.
